Templar's Ledger 1.0.0, the first release: a companion and mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox).

## Getting started

1. Download **`Templars-Ledger-1.0.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere.
2. Double-click **`Start Templar's Ledger.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Ledger's **Options** tab, pick the rule changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Ledger follows it.

The game folder is never changed. The Ledger runs a patched copy of the game, and copies of two of its data files, from its own folder. Your saves keep what you'd expect from play: the items the Ledger hands out, and the XP it gives.

## What's in it

- **The rolls behind the scenes:** attacks, damage, saving throws, magic resistance, initiative, thief skills, character creation and level-up hit points, with each bonus named. Party members' names are in colours of their own, monsters' in red.
- **A party viewer:** THAC0 with each weapon, saves as they stand now, AC and what makes it up, spell slots, thief skills, equipment and active effects.
- **In the game itself**, in its own lettering:
  - THAC0, saves, thief skills and DEX adjustments on the inventory screen;
  - spell slots on the USE screen;
  - each turn's rolls in a pop-up, if you want them;
  - what hurts a monster in the Look box.
- **Dialogue and spells tabs:** every conversation, and what each spell and psionic power really does.
- **Optional AD&D rule changes**, all on by default:
  - helms give AC;
  - boots give a move;
  - two-weapon penalties;
  - the spell save for spells;
  - DEX instead of a doubled d20 against fire, cold and electricity;
  - Cat's Grace, with its own icon;
  - hiding in shadows to backstab (rangers too);
  - thief skills from AD&D's table;
  - half-giants wielding two-handers in one hand;
  - class levels up to 10.
- **New items and thief play:**
  - a Ring of Protection +1 in the arena;
  - gear for Kurzak, Legcrusher and Pehtucl, with icons of their own;
  - the rest of the bone scale armour, with a Bone Helm;
  - Thieves' Tools, and picking anyone's pockets;
  - no thief skill penalty for what a thief holds.
- **A mini-quest:** take the cooked vulture to Dinos.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
